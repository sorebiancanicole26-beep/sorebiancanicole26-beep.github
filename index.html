💡 Key Findings & Correlation Analysis

▶ Speed & Location Insights: The Computer Laboratory exhibits the highest average download speed (82.44 Mbps) and lowest downtime (10.0 mins), whereas the Student Center records the lowest download speed (65.63 Mbps) and highest downtime (28.20 mins).
▶ Device vs. Speed Correlation ($r = -0.009$): Shows almost no linear relationship between connected devices and download speed, proving network bottlenecks depend more on infrastructure/location than mere device load.
▶ Downtime vs. Satisfaction Correlation ($r = -0.748$): Reveals a strong negative correlation, confirming that higher network downtime directly leads to lower user satisfaction ratings across campus.

🛠️ Data-Driven Recommendations

Based on empirical analysis of the 50 WiFi records, the following improvements are recommended:

  • Investigate & Upgrade Student Center Network: Address high downtime (28.20 mins) and lower bandwidth capacity through equipment upgrades.
  • Implement Proactive Downtime Monitoring: Mitigate the strong negative impact of interruptions ($r = -0.748$) on user satisfaction via scheduled maintenance.
  • Optimize Bandwidth Allocation: Base network expansions on location diagnostics rather than raw connected device counts ($r = -0.009$).
