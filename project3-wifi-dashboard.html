💡 Key Findings & Correlation Insights

1. Location Speed Bottlenecks

The Computer Laboratory achieves the highest download speed (82.44 Mbps) with minimal downtime (10 mins), whereas the Student Center suffers from the lowest speed (65.63 Mbps) and highest downtime (28.20 mins) due to high concurrent device density.

2. Connected Devices vs. Speed Correlation ($r = -0.009$)

Shows virtually no linear correlation between device volume and download speed. This proves that network speed degradation is tied primarily to router hardware limits and backbone congestion rather than raw device counts.

3. Downtime vs. Satisfaction Correlation ($r = -0.748$)

Reveals a strong negative correlation ($r = -0.748$). As downtime minutes increase in areas like the Student Center and Main Building, user satisfaction drops sharply, highlighting network stability as the primary driver of campus user morale.

🛠️ Actionable IT Recommendations

1

Student Center & Main Building Upgrade

Deploy high-capacity mesh routers and secondary fiber uplinks to resolve severe downtime spikes exceeding 27 minutes.

2

Proactive Downtime Mitigation

Mitigate the strong negative impact ($r = -0.748$) on satisfaction by scheduling maintenance during non-peak off-hours.

3

Dynamic Bandwidth Throttling

Implement Quality of Service (QoS) rules to prioritize academic research over streaming traffic in heavy-use zones.
