Evidence-Based Strategic Recommendations

Actionable technical interventions derived from empirical ticket metrics and correlation findings.

1 Response Time Optimization

Action: Implement an automated self-service portal for simple Account/Login and password resets.

Evidence: Account/Login requests average 40 minutes response time despite taking only 1.6 hours to resolve.

2 Resolution Efficiency

Action: Provide IT technicians with specialized diagnostic toolkits and targeted training for Hardware and Network fixes.

Evidence: Hardware issues take the longest average resolution (6.0 hrs), followed by Network (4.9 hrs).

3 First-Contact Resolution Focus

Action: Prioritize resolving ticket issues on first contact to avoid multi-day escalations.

Evidence: Strong correlation ($r = -0.59$) proves faster resolution directly yields higher user satisfaction.
